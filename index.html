Stock doesn't leave until the terms are met.

Every distributor in this room has released stock and then spent three months chasing the payment. The usual fix is a policy, and a policy is a conversation your warehouse staff have to win against a customer they like.

We made it a rule the system enforces. Terms unmet, stock does not move. Nobody has to argue, and nobody has to be the bad guy. The only way past it is a named person approving it on the record.
