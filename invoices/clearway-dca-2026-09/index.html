Scope of work · as defined in build plan rev 4

Five phases, delivered in order.

Phase 1 is a complete working tracker. Each subsequent phase removes manual work from a function Phase 1 already performs, so the system is useful from the first phase onward.

1

The tracker

All shipments on a single board. Three status views in DCA's own terms: cargo position, customs entry status and the department currently responsible. The five-day clock, running from actual arrival and counting calendar days, with its status bands. Problems with owners and dates. The attention list, ranked by severity and age. Reports, with delays classified as within or outside DCA's control. Departments and permissions configured as confirmed. The current tracking sheet imported with its history.

2

Alerts

Viber notifications for items already identified by the attention list, so that staff need not be at the screen.

3

Customs status from the VASP

Sent / Lodged, Selected, Final and Paid / Debited recorded automatically on a schedule. InterCommerce first, then CDEC, then eKonek. Credentials are never stored in the system, every check is logged, and the connection suspends itself if logins fail.

4

Cargo status from the shipping line

ETA, actual arrival and container status from the carrier's own tracking. This feed supplies the date on which the five-day clock depends. Where a date is entered manually, the system records its source.

5

The Trackify connection

A one-way, read-only connection from which Trackify reads. One key per customer, disabled until enabled by DCA, with every request logged. Internal statuses, targets, performance, staff and financial data are never shared.

Precondition · sheet review

Before Phase 1 begins, Andar reviews DCA's live tracking sheet against the design and adjusts the design to the real data. Dates are assigned to the phases after this review.

Acceptance standard

Before Phase 1 begins, a one-page pass/fail standard is written for each phase and approved by DCA. A phase is complete when it meets that standard and is in daily use by DCA staff.

Safeguards

A shipment with no recorded arrival is named on the attention list, is never shown with a zero, and is reported on its own line, excluded from every average.

Open points · none affects the price

Whether the arrival day counts as day zero or day one for the five-day target. Whether the Approaching Target threshold is 5 days on the badge and 2 on the attention list, or the same on both. DCA's approximate monthly shipment volume and current tracker size.

Terms

Terms of engagement.

Scope

Andar will design, build and deliver Clearway as described on page 3: the five phases defined in build plan rev 4, the sheet review, the written acceptance standard, import of the current tracker with its history, configuration of departments and users, and hand-over to DCA staff. Where this document and revision 4 differ, this document prevails.

Price and payment

The price is fixed at ₱100,000.00 for the scope in item 01. It is payable in two instalments of ₱50,000.00: the first on acceptance, the second on completion of Phase 1 against the acceptance standard. Each instalment is due within 15 days. Changes to scope are agreed in writing, with a price, before work on them begins.

DCA's rights

DCA receives a perpetual, company-wide licence to use Clearway for its own business, without limit on users and without renewal. All data entered into the system belongs to DCA and may be exported at any time. The system is delivered running on infrastructure under DCA's control.

Andar's rights

Andar retains ownership of the software, its source code and its design, and reserves the right to license, resell and white-label Clearway, and any work derived from it, to other brokerages and businesses under their name or its own. This right is the basis of the early-customer price. It extends to the software only, and is subject to item 05.

Confidentiality

DCA's shipments, clients, staff, departments, documents, reasons, targets, performance figures and tracking sheet are confidential and will not be disclosed to or shared with any other customer. DCA's name and any reference to DCA as a customer are used only with DCA's written consent. The data-sharing agreement for the Trackify connection is a separate document and is executed before that connection is built.

Acceptance

Before Phase 1 begins, Andar prepares a one-page pass/fail standard for each phase, which DCA approves. A phase is complete when it meets that standard and is in daily use by DCA staff. Defects against the standard are corrected by Andar at no charge.

DCA's obligations

DCA provides the full export of its current tracker before the sheet review; the two one-line decisions on the clock and the Approaching Target threshold; written authorisation to query the VASP portals, and a sub-account where the VASP permits one, before Phase 3; and the data-sharing agreement before Phase 5. Delay on DCA's part adjusts dates, not the price.

Third-party costs

Charges levied by third parties are excluded and are paid by DCA directly: hosting and domain, Viber or messaging charges, VASP accounts, and any work on the Trackify side. Andar will advise DCA of each cost before it is incurred. Andar does not modify Trackify.

Support after delivery

Correction of defects against the acceptance standard is included. Ongoing maintenance, changes and further development are available under a separate monthly agreement, which DCA is not obliged to take. Without it, Clearway continues to operate as delivered.

Schedule, validity and law

Dates are assigned to the phases in writing after the sheet review. This proposal is valid for 30 days from 4 September 2026. It is governed by the laws of the Republic of the Philippines. Either party may terminate in writing at the end of any phase, with payment due for phases completed against the standard.
