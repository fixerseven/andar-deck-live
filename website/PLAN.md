# Andar website · content and section plan

Drawing: Company website · Rev C · 2026-09-04
Companion: `website/UX-RESEARCH.md` — interaction design, motion, stack, wireframes.

Tagline is **Make it run.** (not "We make it run"). Rev C changes three things from Rev B. The through-line is now **"your business keeps
moving when you're not in the room"**, with the approval gate as its resolution. Every
card in the rail is a **business**, not a feature. And every line is written the way
Apple writes for people who don't care how it works — *10,000 songs*, never *20 GB*.

---

## 1. Positioning

### The spine

An owner in Cebu built the business by being there. Every question comes to them, every
release waits for them, every mistake finds them. Take a day off and it slows down. That is
not a business running. That is a person running.

> **Make it run.** — so your business keeps moving when you're not there.
> *andar, v. — to run; to operate; of a machine, to be working.*

### The resolution

Running without you in the room does not mean running behind your back. Anything that
touches your money stops and asks you, the way a good assistant would, and it waits.

> **It still asks you first.** You're in the decision, not in the errand.

Freedom up top, control underneath. Those are the owner's two wishes, and they usually
believe they have to choose. The site's job is to show they don't.

### Three things that make it true

| | The line | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **We run businesses of our own.** Both of us show up, every time. | A biography, not a claim. |
| 02 | **We build one thing, and it's running before the end of next month.** | Checkable, and the sample on the page shows the shape. |
| 03 | **It asks you first.** | Nobody else is selling restraint. Restraint is what the owner is buying. |

### Written for

The owner of a Cebu business with ten to two hundred people. Distribution, trading,
brokerage, delivery, export, service. Not technical, not against technology, sold to badly
before. Decides about you in ninety seconds, standing up, on a phone.

### How we write

Like Apple writes: the outcome the reader can picture, never the mechanism, never a unit.

| Don't say | Say |
|---|---|
| Source code is yours · no per-seat licence | **It's yours. Like the truck.** Nothing to rent, nothing that expires, everyone in the company can use it. |
| Approval-gated · nothing automatic touches your money | **It asks you first.** |
| Real-time · true as of a second ago | **As of right now.** |
| In production · used by your staff on an ordinary Tuesday | **Your staff use it every day. Not a demo.** |
| A written pass/fail standard | **We write down what "working" means before we start.** It works, or we're not finished. |
| Diagnostic · Sprint · Retainer | **We come and look · We build it · We keep it running** |
| A written operations map | **A one-page picture of where your hours and your money actually go.** |
| 2–4 weeks to production | **Running in your business before the end of next month.** |
| 5 systems in production | **Five businesses run on what we built. Two of them are ours.** |
| Prototype · drawn, not built | **A working sample. Click it.** |
| Your data, your infrastructure, the keys in your hands | **Your numbers stay with you.** |
| AI, automation, system, platform, solution, transformation | *(nothing — describe what happens instead)* |

The drafting labels (`Sheet 01`, `Detail E · scale 1:1`) stay as visual texture for the
creative-director layer. They never appear in a sentence the owner has to read.

## 4. Section by section

### 00 · Nav

Andar mark. Work · How it goes · Who. One red button: **Book a walk-through**. Far right,
small and live: `Cebu · 14:22`. The clock says *real place, your time zone, reachable now*
without a sentence spent on it. Transparent over the hero; gains a paper fill when the rail pins.

### 01 · Hero

- H1: **Make it run.**
- Sub: *So your business keeps moving when you're not there. Built in Cebu by two people
  who run businesses of their own.*
- Buttons: **Book a walk-through** · **See five businesses that do ↓**
- Small, below the fold line: *andar, v. — to run; to operate; of a machine, to be working.*
- The drawing: four numbered parts of a business — stock, invoice, terms, the gate — as a
  still line drawing, readable in a screenshot. Then it starts: flow lines advance, parts tick
  through their states, and the gate holds while everything upstream keeps moving, until a
  stamp lands and it opens. The position is in the drawing before anyone reads a word.

### 02 · Recognition — one screen, one idea

- H2: **Right now, it runs because you're there.**
- Three lines, set as a list:
  > Every question comes to you.
  > Every release waits for you.
  > Every mistake finds you.
- The turn: **Take a day off and the whole thing slows down. That isn't a business
  running. That's you running.**

No numbers, no fear. The reader supplies the evidence from last week.

### 03 · The rail — six businesses

The reference mechanic (ohmd.com): the section pins, a headline is held behind the cards,
cards travel right to left across it, the section releases when the last card clears.

- Held headline: **It runs. Even when you're not there.**
- Sub: *Five businesses. Two of them are ours.*
- Progress: a datum line, ticked 01–06, in the drawing's idiom.

**Card anatomy**, the same on every card: *the business* (eyebrow) → *what they needed*
(one plain line) → *what changed* (headline) → two or three sentences of what a day looks
like now → a glimpse of the screen their staff use → a truth label (built for ourselves /
running at a client / a working sample).

#### 01 · A hardware distributor · 40 customers on credit · *needed someone to say no for them*
**Stock waits for the money now. And it asks you first.**
A customer who's over their terms asks for a release. Before, your warehouse had to argue.
Now the shelf stays put, your phone gets a message, and nothing moves until you tap yes.
Screen: the picking terminal — *Release held · ₱312,880 overdue · Ask the boss*.
Truth: Built for our own business.

#### 02 · A materials trader · two warehouses · *needed one glance, not three phone calls*
**How much stock do we have? You look at your phone. That's it.**
Stock in both warehouses, money owed to you, money you owe, and anything waiting on your
say-so. As of right now, wherever you are.
Screen: the handset — four numbers and *1 waiting on you*.
Truth: Built for our own business.

#### 03 · A furniture exporter · billing in dollars · *needed to get paid faster*
**The invoice goes out before the container does.**
It's ready the moment the order is, in pesos and dollars at today's rate, and the customer
pays by scanning it. Nobody retypes a number, so nobody gets one wrong.
Screen: the invoice, the QR, *Scan to pay*.
Truth: Built for our own business.

#### 04 · A delivery company · vans and three branches · *needed to stop being the person everyone calls*
**Every problem, written down, with a name on it.**
A van's GPS drops. A branch count is off. A customer wants a receipt reprinted. Each one
lands on a board, somebody's name goes on it, and the owner sees what came in and what's
done — without a single call.
Screen: the board — *14 open · 38 sorted today*.
Truth: Running every day at a Cebu company. Named once they say yes.

#### 05 · A customs brokerage · a dozen importers · *needed every shipment in one place*
**Where's the container? One screen answers it.**
Every shipment moves through eight steps, arrival to delivered. Every document ticked off.
Every errand at the port has a name next to it. Ask forty times a day and get the same
answer every time.
Screen: the command centre. Button: **Click the working sample** (new tab, runs offline).
Truth: A working sample with made-up data. Click it.

#### 06 · Your business
**What's waiting on you right now?**
Tell us. We'll come and look, and we'll say honestly whether there's something worth
building. The one-page picture of your business is yours to keep either way.
Button: **Book a walk-through**.
The rail ends on the visitor's own business. They've just slid through five others and
arrived at theirs.

### 04 · The gate — full width, interactive

- H2: **It still asks you first.**
- Running without you in the room doesn't mean running behind your back. Anything that
  touches your money — releasing stock, giving credit, changing a price — stops and asks.
  It messages you the way a good assistant would, and it waits. All afternoon, if it has to.
- The phone, large and live. *Release stock · Bay 05 · ₱482,400 · Waiting on you.* The
  visitor taps **Yes**; the stamp lands. *Try it again.* Do not time it out; the patience is
  the demonstration.
- Under it: **You're in the decision. Not in the errand.**

### 09 · The ask

- One week. We come to you. You get a one-page picture of where your hours and your money

## 5. Copy rules

1. One idea per screen.
2. Outcomes the reader can picture, never units. *Paid by scanning it*, not *QR-enabled*.
3. Every card is a business first. Name the trade, the size, the thing they needed.
4. The words *AI, automation, system, platform, solution, source code, licence, real-time,
   production, deploy* do not appear on the page.
5. Numbers are ours or they are not there.
6. Name nothing without permission.
7. Read every line aloud to someone who runs a shop. If they ask what a word means, cut it.

## 7. Phases

1. **Foundation** — tokens, type, nav, the hero drawing, the rail with two real cards. If
   the rail doesn't feel inevitable here, change the mechanic before building more.
2. **The page** — remaining cards, the gate, the promises, how it goes, who, ways in, the ask.
   Mobile and reduced-motion built alongside.
3. **Polish** — speed, fonts, and a read-aloud pass with an owner who has never seen the
   deck. If they can't say what Andar does afterwards, the copy failed, not the listener.
