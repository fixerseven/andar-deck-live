01

The line

An owner built the business by being there. The site sells the day they don't have to be, and then shows them they're still the one who says yes.

Every question comes to them. Every release waits for them. Every mistake finds them. Take a day off and the whole thing slows down. That is not a business running. That is a person running.

The promise: Make it run. So your business keeps moving when you're not there.
The resolution: It still asks you first. You're in the decision, not in the errand.

Freedom up top, control underneath. Those are the owner's two wishes, and they usually believe they have to choose. The whole page exists to show they don't. The approval gate is not a feature on a list; it is the answer to the fear the headline raises.

Why it's true · 01

We run businesses of our own

Both of us show up, every time. A biography, not a claim; a freelancer or a dev shop can't copy it.

Why it's true · 02

One thing, before the end of next month

We build one thing and it's running in your business before the end of next month. Checkable, and the working sample on the page shows the shape.

Why it's true · 03

It asks you first

Nobody else in this category is selling restraint, and restraint is what the owner is actually buying.

Written for the owner of a Cebu business with ten to two hundred people. Distribution, trading, brokerage, delivery, export, service. Not technical, not against technology, sold to badly before. Decides about you in ninety seconds, standing up, on a phone.

11

UI and UX · the calls

From the full specification in the repo (website/UX-RESEARCH.md, twelve verified reference sites, code sketches, wireframes). The decisions that matter, in one screen.

The rail

Pin and travel, on top of a real scroller

GSAP ScrollTrigger pins the section and moves the cards, layered on a native horizontal scroll container. No JavaScript, a phone, or reduced-motion all get the plain scroller for free. The feel is one number: scroll one screen, the cards travel one screen. Anything faster is what people mean by "hijacked". Seven cards maximum.

The hero

"Cold start"

Three concepts ranked. The winner is the still assembly drawing that begins running, with the datum line becoming the page's progress bar. The headline text paints first; the drawing follows. No hero photo, no video.

Motion

Seven named moves

Draw, settle, stamp, travel, tick, run, handoff. Each with its easing and duration, each marked functional or decorative, never more than twelve moving at once. The site feels like one instrument, not a collection of effects.

Type

Barlow for everyone

Drop the local FF DIN fallback: it made the founders' screens differ from every juror's. Ship Barlow Semi Condensed and Barlow, or license FF DIN properly. Body text scales with the reader's settings; the deck's viewport-scaled type is kept only inside the card screens.

Stack

Astro on GitHub Pages

Static pages, no JavaScript shipped unless a section needs it, GSAP for the rail only, no smooth-scroll library (two scroll systems is the top source of jank). GitHub Pages holds; the founders keep publishing the way they already do.

Budget

Fast enough to feel like an instrument

First paint under 1.5 seconds, nothing shifts, taps respond in under a tenth of a second, under 300 KB for the first screen. Awwwards weights usability at 30%, so keyboard and screen-reader access on the rail is load-bearing, not optional.
